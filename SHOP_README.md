# Cutter handoff, R5C

Use only REAPER_R5C_PLY_PANEL_01.dxf and REAPER_R5C_PLY_PANEL_02.dxf. R5C uses the corrected A26 motor holes: four 3.2 mm holes on a 19 x 19 mm square. All production panels are clean cut-only re-nests; earlier panel files are superseded.

Wood: **two** 609.6 x 457.2 mm panels, nominal 6 mm, face grain along the long/X edge. Foam: **five** 600 x 457.2 mm bed loads from **three** 1000 x 600 mm sheets, with at least 5 mm edge and part spacing. FW01, FW02 and FW07 stay whole. Foam cannot be 609.6 mm wide because the owned sheet is 600 mm.

Open each R5C plywood and foam panel DXF once at **100% in millimetres**. Each production DXF contains only closed outlines on `CUT`: no labels, marks or stock border. The separate `REAPER_R5C_PLY_FIT_COUPON_ONLY.dxf` is the first test job. Part IDs and hand-mark lines appear only on PNG maps and in the PDF. No kerf compensation is baked in. Measure actual stock and sample fit, then set laser process for this exact material with the shop. Preserve the foam outside paper; form and trim after cutting.

The 37 wooden aircraft parts include paired lap plates for the keel and two rear-spar joins. The separate plywood fit coupon is not an aircraft part. The 13 foam patterns produce 15 blanks: FW12 and FW13 each need two layers. No thin CAD-face strips are separate cuts.
