# Print order

Print the 14 parts in Print_Schedule.csv, one each. Reuse the existing N1 nose. AR, AL, TRC and TLC are now foamboard: do not print the earlier moving controls or use their old finishing templates.

Use the two model-only layouts under Batches/ for the ten small parts: four supported horns, then four flat backers and two servo cradles. Apply the per-object settings in the schedule; the 3MF files do not contain a printer/material profile. Print by layer, with every object at 100% scale. Trial a horn/backer fit before the remaining batch.

Print FCF, FTF, AHR and AHL separately in their reviewed orientations. Use the revised FTF with local inner relief for the 6 mm plywood tail parts. Inspect its thin opening lips after support removal. The upright fixed rails need about 274.1 mm usable height plus machine allowances; confirm the actual printer. Do not infer its exact height from the report that the nose fitted.

Model dimensions exclude any additional printer exclusion zones, purge lines or safety allowance. Reference slicing estimates do not prove adhesion, strength or machine compatibility. No machine-specific G-code is supplied.
