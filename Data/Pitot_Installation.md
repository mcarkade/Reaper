# Pitot: simple tape-on installation trial

Use the actual probe for a reversible external dry-fit first. Tape, a thin non-slip pad and loose hose-retaining tape loops avoid a new mount and the long internal route. Keep N1 and the released plywood unchanged. This is a trial method, not a physically verified mount.

## Five assembly steps

1. **Position the real probe.** With the battery out, hold the probe outside the left side of N1, pointing straight forward. Move it until the rearmost side hole is at least 10 mm ahead of the foremost nose point. Keep the front opening and every side hole exposed.
2. **Trial two tape bands.** Put both bands on plain shaft behind all pressure holes. Use a thin removable non-slip pad. Check that both bands support the shaft on the real nose curve without rocking, bending it or pulling it sideways. Do not drill, slot or sand N1. Adjust the position before cutting anything.
3. **Route outside.** Label both hoses total/static at both ends. Attach them loosely along the outside, across to the removable foam cover, then enter its side seam beside the open rear edge of battery shelf FBT. Dry-fit a small rounded foam-only relief if needed. Keep hoses out of tape closure lines and never clamp them between covers.
4. **Connect under the shelf.** With covers open, lead the pair around FBT's open rear edge to the ASPD board underneath. Follow the actual kit's port diagram. Leave smooth bends and enough slack to lift N1/FSU1 and disconnect the labelled hoses if necessary. Measure and cut hoses only after this fitted route is established.
5. **Prove access and retention.** Refit covers and battery, check the straps and battery removal, then gently disturb the probe. It must remain aligned without slipping. Inspect for flattened hoses and leaks; check still-air zero and response to gentle airflow using the electronics guide. Remove/refit the covers and repeat.

## What the picture means

The shell and shelf outlines come from delivered CAD. The probe, tape bands and dashed hose line illustrate the assembly method; they are not dimensions or verified hardware positions. Side-view hardware is drawn offset for readability. The sensor rectangle is the nominated board allowance, not measured nipple locations.

The nose is strongly tapered. Its approximate half-width changes from 23 mm at 20 mm behind the tip to 44 mm at 60 mm behind it. Tape does not make a straight shaft conform to that curve. Fit two stable retention areas using the actual probe; check forward alignment from above and from the side. If thin padding cannot support it, revise the position rather than building a large improvised stand-off or forcing the tube sideways.

## Why the internal tab is an alternative

The existing FBT tab is inside the shell. A 4 mm diameter test shaft can pass through lower foam piece FNG1 without intersecting N1 or wood. Its foam contact lies approximately 41–67 mm behind the nose tip. Two internal retention bands would require substantial reach: about 110–130 mm from those bands to the rearmost side hole. These are geometry requirements, not the owned probe's dimensions.

A 3 mm hose gauge was also routed internally through the existing front-former opening and around the shelf rear; its straight segment lengths total 398 mm. Simple straight routing collided with wood. The corrected seven-segment gauge clears delivered solids, but it does not establish two-hose packing, bend radius, fittings or service slack. Do not use 398 mm as a hose cut length. Internal coordinates and gauge results remain separate audit evidence; this more involved route is not the default beginner method.

## Check on the real hardware

Measure probe diameter, available grip length, hole positions, rear fittings, hose sizes and supplied lengths. Check tape/pad grip, the actual curved contact areas, foam-seam clearance, fitted bends and cover service slack. Neither diagram nor CAD establishes pressure integrity, mounting strength, calibrated airspeed or flight acceptance.

The 10 mm forward-hole target follows the installation example in [ArduPilot's airspeed guide](https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/airspeed.html), checked 23 September 2026. It is not proof of undisturbed airflow on this airframe. The Matek product page returned HTTP 403 during this audit, so no new kit dimensions or port assignments are inferred from it.
