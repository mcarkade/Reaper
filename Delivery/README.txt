Reaper R5F print and assembly files

The two A44 outer ribs keep the original shape but now print in PLA+ with four walls and 100% rectilinear infill. A11 is the unchanged left aileron and A21 the unchanged right aileron that were missed earlier. The A26 mount now prints in ASA: its corrected holes follow the EMAX drawing's 19 mm bolt circle and its frame seats match the nominal 6 mm skeleton. The old wooden mount is superseded; no motor laser DXF is included.

The printed parts seat on the matching wood faces and sockets shown in the new guide. Dry-fit the real rods, frame and hardware first, prepare only the bond patches, then use a coupon-proven adhesive suitable for the print material and wood. Keep joints square through the adhesive's full cure, leave access to screws and cables, and check the restraints before closing skins.

Use Reaper_Beginner_Assembly_R5F.pdf from your existing cut/printed parts onward. Read Print_Settings.csv before slicing. The STLs do not store settings. The upright unchanged ailerons need about 271 mm printer height; check the actual machine. A26 requires an ASA-capable enclosed printer and the exact spool profile, which are not yet confirmed.

CAD/mesh/slicing checks do not prove physical fit or flight readiness. Check motor seating and safe screw depth, ASA-to-wood bonding, clamp creep, vibration/temperature and actual thrust/torque. Measure complete aircraft mass/CG and qualify the 4S/9045 three-blade/40A ESC propulsion. The 20%-chord CG is only a geometric starting point. Landing gear remains deferred until after midsems, and flight acceptance remains gated.
