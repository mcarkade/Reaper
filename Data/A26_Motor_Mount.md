# A26 motor mount: current status

The corrected A26 geometry has already been submitted for PLA printing as a fit test. If that fit is correct, the user plans a plywood replacement. Its conditional DXF is kept locally and has not been released for cutting. The previous ASA choice was an unverified material recommendation; it is not the available shop material. Current fit success is not recorded.

The documented motor is EMAX ECO II 2807 1300KV. Its [manufacturer drawing](EMAX_ECOII_2807_Manufacturer_Drawing.jpg) shows four M3 holes on a 19 mm diameter bolt circle (13.435 mm adjacent spacing), not a 19 mm square. The corrected CAD uses 3.2 mm clearance holes, nominal 6 mm side/keel seats and the original shaft-axis position. Actual motor seating and usable screw engagement remain hardware checks.

The original build uses nominal 6 mm plywood for this plate. The corrected geometry is a single planar 6 mm plate, suitable as a conditional one-piece cut profile; actual plywood thickness/condition and shop kerf must be confirmed. Replace the old square-pattern plate rather than redrilling it.

The existing A62_A aft projection extends 13.015 mm past the motor face. The guide and CAD/Secondary_Removal/A62_A_AFT_TRIM.step identify its local trim flush to world Y582.650 mm; retain the forward joint and surrounding frame.

PLA fit checking establishes geometry, not loaded motor-mount qualification. Neither the PLA prototype nor an untested plywood replacement is cleared for flight by CAD or a successful fit alone.
