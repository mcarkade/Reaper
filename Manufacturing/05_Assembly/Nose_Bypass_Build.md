# Nose gear joint

The split spine leaves room for the steering shaft. Two continuous plywood cheeks and four M2 bolts join it around the shaft.

## Parts and dimensions

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Spine | A62F and A62R, 5 mm plywood; 3.4 mm gap centred at Y = -293.65 mm |
| Cheeks | A63/A64, two identical 72 × 10.7 × 2.5 mm aircraft-plywood parts; grain along 72 mm |
| Mount / bearing | Revised A37 and A09; 3 mm nose wire through the bearing and steering clamp |
| Inner bolts | Two M2 × 40–45 mm starting lengths, at Y = -302 and -282 mm |
| Outer bolts | Two M2 × 16 mm starting lengths, at Y = -320 and -264 mm |
| Bolt holes | 2.2 mm diameter, through X; Z = -37.964 mm |

Use washers on both sides and locking nuts. Select final bolt lengths from the actual stack, with 1–2 threads beyond the locknut. Do not substitute soft balsa or self-tapping screws into end grain.

## Assemble

1. Align A62F/R on a flat jig with the shaft gap centred correctly.
2. Fit one cheek each side and seat them in the A37 pockets.
3. Install all four bolts loosely. Inner bolts pass through the mount; outer bolts clamp the wood stack.
4. Fit A09 and the nose wire. Check straight spine alignment and free rotation.
5. Bond wood interfaces, keeping glue out of the shaft passage. Tighten enough to seat the stack without crushing it.
6. Recheck rotation after cure. Fit the steering clamp/collar; trim the upper wire only after full engagement.

Keep the joint accessible. Inspect bolt bearing, plywood, printed mount and adhesive under the intended gear deflection before flight.
