# Reaper REV4 plywood cutting

Cut all 2 numbered panel DXFs once. Together they contain 87 aircraft parts and five separate setup test pieces. Use nominal 6 mm plywood panels 609.6 x 457.2 mm (2 x 1.5 ft), with face grain along the 609.6 mm X edge. Do not rotate individual profiles across the grain to pack them tighter.

1. Import in millimetres at 100% scale. Stock borders and labels are references, not cuts.
2. Cut TEST_CUT_FIRST on panel 01. Measure actual stock thickness, slot fit and the three-layer fit stack before proceeding. Slot labels 5.8/6.0/6.2/6.4/18.3 are nominal dimensions, not an automatic clearance recommendation.
3. Cut CUT_AIRCRAFT only after test fit is satisfactory. No kerf compensation is built into these profiles; choose it with the operator from the test result.
4. Keep every ID attached to its part. Assemble all frame and wing joint doublers; a butt joint alone is not the specified joint. See [frame joints](../Data/Frame_Joints.md) and the main Build Guide.

Part spacing is 6 mm and edge margin is at least 10 mm. The sheet map identifies every piece. Physical fit, cured joint strength, actual mass and balance remain required prototype checks. Use the current REV4 set together; do not substitute earlier long-frame or balsa patterns.
