# Plywood

25 profiles make 37 installed pieces: 24 nominal 5 mm, 6 at 3 mm and 7 at 2.5 mm. The two A44 ribs are finished to 4.990 mm.

[Quantities and notes](manufacturing_register.csv) · [Individual DXFs](Individual_DXF/README.md) · [Sheet layouts](Sheet_Layouts/README.md) · [Fit coupons](Fit_Coupons/README.md)

Cut **CUT** only. Other layers are labels, stock or sanding references. Preserve millimetre scale and grain. Run the thickness/slot coupon with the actual stock and process first.

H05B parts are control-horn backing blanks; drill to the purchased horn template after checking the hinge axis. Do not drill a guessed pattern.
