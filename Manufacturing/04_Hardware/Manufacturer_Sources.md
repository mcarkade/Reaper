# Product references

- [Emax ECO II 2807](https://emaxmodel.com/products/emax-eco-ii-series-2807-1300kv-1700kv-1500kv-brushless-motor-for-rc-drone-fpv-racing) and the [mounting drawing](EMAX_Official_Mounting_Drawing.jpg): four M3 threads on a 19 mm bolt circle, 33.9 mm diameter, 34 mm overall length and M5 propeller thread. Adjacent mounting-hole spacing is 13.435 mm, not a 19 mm square. Measure thread engagement on the actual motor.
- [Matek F405-WING V2](https://www.mateksys.com/?portfolio=f405-wing-v2), [M9N-5883](https://www.mateksys.com/?portfolio=m9n-5883) and [ASPD-4525](https://www.mateksys.com/?portfolio=aspd-4525): use the exact model's connector labels and mounting information.
- [TowerPro MG90S](https://towerpro.com.tw/product/mg90s-3/): nominal 13.4 g per servo. Verify the delivered body and ear dimensions against the mounting coupon/dry fit.
- [FliteBoard Pro](https://www.vortex-rc.com/product/fliteboard-pro1000x600mm-20-sheet-pack/): 5 mm paper-faced XPS, nominal 400 GSM. A 1000 x600 mm sheet is nominally 240 g before cutting. Weigh actual material and finished panels.
- [Aluminium bend-radius reference](https://www.cumberlandmetals.com/aluminum/minimum-bend-radii/): the shop must qualify its actual material and forming process.

The selected powertrain is Emax 2807 1300 KV, Gemfan 9045CL three-blade, 4S battery and 40 A ESC. This is the build assumption; current, thrust and temperature are measured before flight.
