# Six-millimetre plywood wing candidate

Use the current cut profiles and finished STEP models together. Digital checks do not establish joint strength or flight readiness.

Each half-wing retains its whole 5 × 1000 mm carbon rod. Do not cut, butt-join, or shorten either rod. Retain the removable root arrangement and its three 6 mm laminates.

The lower cap has a 10 × 6 mm ordinary section. Wider 20 mm sections retain the root interface, support the web joint, and keep the existing servo tray seat. The two cap halves meet near span station X625 mm. Two 120 × 5 × 6 mm side strips span that joint, with 60 mm bonded to each half. Keep all four pieces on the same lower datum in a straight jig. The two glue edges provide 720 mm² of geometric contact per half; the butt ends only locate the pieces.

The vertical web is 6 mm plywood, with its joint at X375 mm. A 100 mm lap plate on each face spans X325–425 mm. The wider cap pad supports both plates. The two joints are deliberately separated along the span. Face grain runs spanwise in every cap, web and lap piece. Rib face grain runs chordwise.

Cut the stock DXFs at 100% from nominal 6 mm plywood. First qualify the laser settings on a coupon from the same sheet, then measure and dry-fit the actual thickness. Char, glue gaps, rough edges and unknown plywood quality can dominate joint performance. The supplied stock and finished STEP shapes are different wherever secondary shaping is required.

Use the finished STEP and the full-length rod in a jig to shape the rod seat and the web's lower bevel. A 5 mm former defines the groove diameter; do not confuse diameter with radius. Do not laser a round rod groove through the stock. Enlarge only the existing internal relief in the root laminates; preserve their original external tongue and receiver-bearing surfaces. No extra fasteners are introduced.

The eight rib segments are complete through-cut 6 mm profiles. The four wing foam outer patterns stay the same; remove only the local internal foam interference shown by the finished model. Preserve the outside paper and inspect the remaining skin. Existing glue clearance around ribs still requires a continuous qualified fillet.

The geometric contact areas are not glue strength ratings. The eventual report's bending calculation assumes perfect bonds and does not establish peel, torsion, local buckling, joint slip, fatigue, material strength or impact resistance. Test representative paired cap-edge and web-face lap coupons, then proof-test the assembled wing and receiver with the final mass/load plan. Measure completed mass and CG before propulsion or flight testing.
