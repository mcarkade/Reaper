- [NASA_LIFT](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/lift-equation/): Lift equation; CL depends on actual shape and flow. Does not supply Reaper CLmax.
- [NASA_MOTION](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/equations-of-motion/): Net force/mass gives acceleration; constant-force kinematics. Does not supply Reaper thrust.
- [EMAX](https://shop.emax-usa.com/collections/brushless-motors/products/eco-ii-2807-brushless-motor-1300kv-1500kv-1700kv): Official ECOII2807 page describes 7-inch-prop application; no exact owned9-inch/4S performance qualification.
- [GEMFAN](https://www.gemfanhobby.com/gemfan-9045-3-glass-fiber-nylon.html): 9045-3 is9inch,4.5inch pitch,3blades,M5; compatible motor range listed2808–3118. Exact ownedprop variant still checked on its packaging.
- [ARDUPILOT_TAKEOFF](https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/takeoff-mode.html): Runway alignment/heading and wheeled steering configuration matter. Autopilot takeoff mode is not qualification of this fixed-wheel prototype.
- [FAA_FACTORS](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aip_html/part2_enr_section_5.3.html): Takeoff performance depends on runway condition/slope, weight, wind, elevation and temperature. No full-scale rule of thumb is adopted for this model.
