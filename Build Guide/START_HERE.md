# Current build documents

Start with **Complete_Build_Guide.pdf** for the full illustrated manual, with bookmarks and a contents page. The separate **01_Airframe_Manufacturing_and_Assembly.pdf** and **Electronics_Build_Guide.pdf** remain available for quick reference.

The teammate ZIP is in the sibling Manufacturing folder. It contains the consolidated manual, manufacturing files, quantities and digital verification report. All older AI packages are archived outside it.

The other PDFs here are reading guides, sheet overviews, part atlases, foam assembly/forming illustrations and verification evidence. Actual-size cutting and tiled templates are in Manufacturing/Package/Print_Templates and in the ZIP, not here. Print templates at Actual Size /100%, and measure their calibration marks. Overview pages are not cutting templates.

This is a prototype fabrication handoff. Run its shop coupons, complete dry fitting, and perform the documented physical checks. The provisional balance station and owned propulsion combination are not a flight release.
