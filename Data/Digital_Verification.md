# Digital verification

REV4 contains 133 aircraft CAD identities, 87 plywood cut profiles, 39 foam patterns and 14 new print meshes. N1 is reused unchanged. The old long body/beam parts, balsa orders and moving-control print meshes are removed.

Use [Integration_Checks.json](Integration_Checks.json) for identity, source-hash and numerical checks; [Geometry_Verification.json](Geometry_Verification.json) for geometry evidence; [Release_Verification.json](Release_Verification.json) for final output hashes. The shop package has its own panel and source-profile checks. Print models were reference-sliced; those estimates are not measured parts or actual-printer approval.

Physical stock fit, glued joints, thin foam pockets, support removal, loaded controls, measured mass/CG, propulsion and flight remain open in [Physical_Checks.csv](Physical_Checks.csv).
