# Join the short plywood frame pieces

Use the 12 named pieces below. A doubler is a reinforcing strip glued across a joint. Both doublers are required at each joint; the touching butt ends only locate the halves.

Measure stations aft from the **front tip of FK_F**, not the printed nose tip.

| Member | Join these halves | Joint station | Reinforcement |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Centre keel | FK_F + FK_A | 460 mm | FK_DL and FK_DR, one on each side; 60 mm overlap onto each half |
| Left rail | FLL_F + FLL_A | 625 mm | FLL_DT above and FLL_DB below; 45 mm overlap onto each half |
| Right rail | FLR_F + FLR_A | 637.5 mm | FLR_DT above and FLR_DB below; 45 mm overlap onto each half |

1. Cover a flat board with release film and mark a straight alignment line. Dry-fit each matching pair.
2. Remove loose laser char from bonding faces without changing the outline. Test the chosen structural wood adhesive on the actual plywood. Follow its surface-preparation and cure instructions; do not use hot glue for these joints.
3. Hold the keel halves in their original alignment. Glue FK_DL/DR on the two side faces. Their top edges sit 11 mm below the level top-face datum of the original rails; their bottom edges sit 31 mm below it. Use the guide close-up and finished STEP for placement around the lightening holes.
4. Keep each rail's original top faces level. Glue its top and bottom strips across the joint. Do not let a strip tilt the rail or leave a gap under it.
5. Clamp gently in alignment and allow a full cure before adding crosspieces or removing the jig. Keep receiver contacts, strap slots and service routes free of glue.
6. Check both reinforcement pieces, straightness and complete bonds. Qualify a representative joint and the assembled frame before loading the battery, wing or motor.

The CAD shows positive contact and clearance. It does not establish the strength of real plywood, charred surfaces, glue or cured joints. See [the build guide](../Build_Guide.pdf), page 46, for the illustrated placement.
