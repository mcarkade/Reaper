# Workshop files

Follow [Reaper_Beginner_Assembly_R5F.pdf](Reaper_Beginner_Assembly_R5F.pdf) and identify parts with [Parts.csv](Data/Parts.csv).

Keep the plywood and foam already cut. Current [plywood maps and DXFs](Plywood) omit the superseded wood A44 ribs and A26 mount; they are not a request to recut the skeleton. The guide shows the local rear-keel trim. [Foam jobs](Foam) are unchanged, including the ROOT/TIP joins if those skins were cut separately.

The [print list](Print/README.md) identifies the two A44 ribs, missed left/right ailerons and A26 fit geometry. The shop is handling the actual PLA slicing and supports. A26's PLA fit result is pending; its separate plywood option will be released only after fit and actual stock thickness are confirmed.

DXFs use millimetres at 100% scale, with cutting geometry on CUT. The shop accounts for its measured kerf and actual stock. Reference STL filenames/settings are not embedded slicer instructions.
