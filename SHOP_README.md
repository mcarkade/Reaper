# Cutter handoff

Wood: **two** 609.6 x 457.2 mm panels, nominal 6 mm, face grain along the long/X edge. Foam: **five** 600 x 457.2 mm bed loads from **three** 1000 x 600 mm sheets (the fifth load is sparse; use offcut where it fits). Foam cannot be 609.6 mm wide because the owned sheet is 600 mm.

Open each numbered panel DXF once at **100% in millimetres**. Cut only `CUT` for the aircraft; on plywood cut `TEST_CUT_FIRST` first. `LABEL_DO_NOT_CUT`, `MARK_DO_NOT_CUT` and `STOCK_DO_NOT_CUT` are references. No kerf compensation is baked in. Measure actual stock and sample fit, then set laser process for this exact material with the shop. Preserve the foam outside paper; form and trim after cutting.

The 37 wooden aircraft parts include both lap plates for each of three unavoidable long-member joins. The two plywood test pieces are not aircraft parts. The 13 foam patterns produce 15 blanks: FW12 and FW13 each need two layers. No thin CAD-face strips are separate cuts.
