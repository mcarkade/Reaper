# Reaper prototype — REV4

**Use 2 plywood panels, each 609.6 × 457.2 × nominal 6 mm, plus three 1000 × 600 × nominal 5 mm foamboards. No balsa is required.** Plywood face grain runs along the 609.6 mm edge. The small-panel set replaces the old large-sheet REV3 layout.

![Aircraft with three-blade 9045 propeller and basic gear](Illustrations/Assembly.png)

Start with the [illustrated build guide](Build_Guide.pdf) or [text guide](Build_Guide.md). It covers the performance assumptions, parts, assembly, electronics placement, every connection, calibration and ground checks. Each wooden part has its own consistent wood shade.

- [Plywood panel 1](Workshop_Plywood_REV4/REAPER_REV4_PLY_PANEL_01.dxf) — cut once.
- [Plywood panel 2](Workshop_Plywood_REV4/REAPER_REV4_PLY_PANEL_02.dxf) — cut once.

Read the [shop instructions](Workshop_Plywood_REV4/SHOP_INSTRUCTIONS.md). Cut the five test pieces first. The complete plywood set contains **87 aircraft pieces**, including the joints needed for short stock. Do not cut every panel twice or combine them on one small sheet.

The ailerons and both moving V-tail controls are foamboard. Their horns and upper backing plates are printed. The three simple gear legs are plywood; fit the supplied wheel/axle assemblies using measured, load-spreading attachments. Keep the existing battery shelf and two Velcro straps.

Estimated flying mass is **2.66 kg**. Estimated stall is **39–50 km/h** under the stated assumptions. Flight time depends on measured current; 15–17 minutes is only the 15 A planning example. Required runway length remains unknown until propulsion and rolling tests. These are not flight-tested values.
