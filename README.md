All geometry uses millimetres. Three plain wooden legs connect the body to the owned wheel/axle assemblies; the owner will handle attachment details. Wheel dimensions and offsets remain workshop measurements. The propeller shown is an illustrative purchased-hardware reference, not a printable design. Digital verification does not establish structural strength, measured CG, propulsion margin or flight readiness.
