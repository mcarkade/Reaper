# Reaper R5: original-style prototype

Start with [Build_Guide.pdf](Build_Guide.pdf) for the illustrated build and checks. This package follows the [original Onshape model](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/da0c1a9f98b6358f736153b1/w/040544fbf77b8cf6608e2eb9/e/28c526eeb45b3f50fa69269f) and [Michael Rechtin's build video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-Q08HQq7fM), adapted locally for owned 6 mm plywood, 5 mm foamboard, PLA+, two whole 5 x 1000 mm solid carbon rods, the Emax motor and printed aileron hinge fit. N1 is already printed. Landing gear is bought and attached by the team after its dimensions are known.

- [SHOP_README.md](SHOP_README.md): exact panel sizes, grain and cutter layers.
- `Plywood/REAPER_R5_PLY_PANEL_01–02.dxf`: **37 installed wood pieces + 2 test pieces**, two 2 x 1.5 ft panels.
- `Foam/REAPER_R5_FOAM_PANEL_01–05.dxf`: **13 patterns / 15 blanks**, three full foam sheets; one continuous lower-body wrap and whole fixed-tail skins.
- `Print/Individual/`: **14 new print parts**, plus one fit coupon; `Print/Batches/` groups compatible small parts.
- `CAD/Reaper_R5_Assembly.step`, `CAD/Parts/`, `Data/Parts.csv`: source-style part placement and identification.
- `Data/Calculation_Summary.json`: conditional weight, balance, stall, endurance and runway scenarios.
- `Data/Physical_Checks.csv`: measurements and acceptance still required on the real build.

Digital geometry and sliced print checks do not establish plywood strength, foam forming success, actual printer compatibility, servo power, propulsion, balance or flight readiness. The builder records these on the aircraft before flight. The design and video credit remain with the original creator.
