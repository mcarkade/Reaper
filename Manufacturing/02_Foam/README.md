# Foam

Use 5 mm FliteBoard Pro paper-faced XPS, 1000 × 600 mm sheets. The set has 33 patterns for 35 blanks; follow [quantities](Foam_Manifest.csv).

1. Read the [forming instructions](FOAM_FORMING_GUIDE.md) before cutting.
2. Use [individual DXFs](DXF), [sheet layouts](Sheet_Layouts) or [paper templates](../Print_Templates/README.md).
3. Match panel IDs to the [assembly map](../../Build%20Guide/Reference/Foam_Assembly_Map.pdf), then dry-form and trim as instructed.

Preserve the outside paper on curved skins. Inside-paper removal, core relief, seams and bevels are part of the forming method. Tail control surfaces use two laminated 5 mm layers. Do not resize patterns for different stock.

Knife cutting is the default. Laser cutting requires workshop approval for this exact XPS product and extraction process. Verify foam-compatible adhesive on scrap.

[Tail forming tools](TOOLING_DXF) are removable tools, not installed parts. Complete a forming coupon and full dry-fit before bonding.
