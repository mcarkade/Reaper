# A26 motor mount

Current R5G is nominal 6 mm plywood with no motor screw holes, as requested for actual-motor hand marking. The shaft relief and source axis at X0/Z-2.5 mm are retained; nominal keel/side receiving regions are 6 mm. A26 is included in the single combined plywood file. The earlier square-pattern plate and printed fit prototypes are superseded references.

The documented motor is EMAX ECO II 2807 1300KV. Its [manufacturer drawing](EMAX_ECOII_2807_Manufacturer_Drawing.jpg) specifies four M3 mounting holes on a 19 mm diameter bolt circle: adjacent centres are 13.435 mm, not a 19 mm square. Align the real motor and mark the pattern only after the complete A26/A43/A62_A/printed-tail dry-fit proves seating, assembly order and tool access. The current DXF deliberately has zero motor screw holes; the centre shaft relief is not a mounting hole.

The frame joint is chiefly the forward-face bond and keel seat; do not mistake the plate's flank recesses for fully captured load-bearing tongues. Proof the real attachment and load path. Digital installed interference and sampled aft insertion checks passed, while a vertical drop is blocked. They do not establish the actual plywood fit or bonding strength. User-reported incorrect physical frame fit remains a physical acceptance gate until this exact replacement is dry-fitted.

A62_A's current stock length is 482.650 mm, flush at Y582.650. Its old 13.015 mm aft projection has already been removed in the current cutting profile: do not trim a further 13.015 mm.

Choose motor screws from the actual plate/washer stack and measured safe thread depth. EMAX supplies M3×8/M3×10 screws, but their nominal protrusions through a 6 mm plate are not proof of adequate engagement or winding clearance. Confirm seating, shaft/boss clearance, screw bottoming, head support, real driver approach, wiring and later service access. The actual hardware-length checks, loaded thrust/torque joint tests and propulsion thermal/vibration tests have not been performed. Do not run a propeller until physical retention and the guide's safety gates pass.
