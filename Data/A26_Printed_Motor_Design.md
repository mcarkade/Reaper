# Printed A26 motor mount / R5F design and acceptance

A26 is now a printed ASA motor firewall, replacing the obsolete wooden plate. Its current installed STEP retains the source outline, motor axis and 6 mm interface thickness. The motor holes are four 3.2 mm clearance holes on the official EMAX ECO II 2807 19 mm pitch circle: adjacent centres 13.435 mm. The two source 5 mm flank receiving slots are locally opened to nominal 6 mm to match the CAD rear frame. The central approximately 12 mm keyhole and nominal 6 mm keel slot remain. No guessed clearance, extra bracket or metal-backing envelope is added.

Print A26_ASA_6W_100RECT_NOSUP_L0p2_B8.stl in ASA with 6 walls, 100% rectilinear infill, 0.2 mm layers, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 8 mm brim and supports off. The forward broad face is on the bed and the motor face is upward. The 47.545 x 39.841 x 6 mm STL is a 100%-scale prototype; do not scale to cure fit. Actual ASA spool and an enclosed, ventilated printer capable of its temperatures must be established before printing. Prusament's reference settings are around 260 C nozzle and 110 C bed; they are not a production profile for the unknown owner's printer. ASA warp can prevent motor flush seating: verify flatness and holes after cooldown.

This is a deliberate conservative solid plate rather than sparse fill in a copied wood part. Full material under screw contact resists infill collapse; 6 walls and continuous planar toolpaths distribute in-plane motor torque around the apertures. Broad-face printing gives a flat mounting surface and keeps the wood-seat/keyhole details supported by the bed. Its remaining weak directions are explicitly unresolved: axial thrust bends the plate and loads layer bonds normal to the print plane, fastener preload can creep or crush plastic, and vibration can fatigue both the plate and adhesive joint. No load capacity is established by the source STEP or material data.

ASA is selected instead of owned PLA+ for heat resistance, toughness and outdoor stability. The manufacturer's specimen HDT is 93 C at 0.45 MPa but 86 C at 1.80 MPa, density 1.07 g/cm3 and interlayer adhesion 11 ± 1 MPa. These values are not an allowable mount temperature or an aircraft strength rating; spool/process variation and local stress matter. Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/prusa3d-content-prod-14e8-wordpress-prusament-prod/2024/05/bc8551ef-tds_prusament-asa_2024_en.pdf

Motor fastening uses four M3 screws into the actual metal motor base. Use metal washers under heads to spread load only after their diameter and the full screw/washer/plate stack fit the surrounding wood and a straight driver path. Verify that every washer seats on supported plastic and does not bridge the shaft relief. Actual safe thread depth and underside boss/protrusion are not dimensioned; measure them and dry-fit the motor flush. M3x8 or M3x10 are not approved automatically. No torque value is invented. Check for clamp relaxation after dwell and load/temperature testing; do not rely on screw-head impressions as retention.

The ASA mount receives A43's aft wood and A62's keel at their source locations. Sand only intended bond surfaces, remove dust and test an epoxy specifically rated for ASA and wood on representative coupons. Wood glue is not suitable evidence of adhesion to ASA. Cure the chosen product for its full label time while the motor plate is held square; keep the motor removed during bonding. No hidden unmeasured reinforcement or hardware is assumed. Confirm that all source joint faces meet, the base seats flush and motor/lead access remains serviceable.

Trim only A62_A's existing 13.015 mm aft projection flush with the motor mounting face, world Y582.650 mm; leave all wood forward of that plane unchanged. CAD/Secondary_Removal/A62_A_AFT_TRIM.step identifies the precise removal. The projection formerly overlapped the manufacturer's motor envelope by 823.136 mm3; zero digital overlap after trimming is not physical hardware acceptance. Do not redo completed cutting or reuse the square-pattern wood motor plate. The current plywood panels omit A26 and both A44 ribs.

Physical gates before powered propulsion or flight: actual receiving-joint plywood thickness and dry fit; four motor holes and boss flush seating; safe M3 engagement and supported washers; representative ASA/wood bond proof; actual axial thrust and torque load proof of the complete rear structure; vibration and screw-clamp dwell checks; actual mount temperature and dimensional/clamp creep under sustained intended load. The 4S/9045 three-blade/40 A ESC combination still needs measured current, thrust and temperature qualification. Neither CAD closure, slicing nor bench motor spin establishes flight readiness. Record measured results and stop if fit, bond, cracking, clamp or thermal tests fail.
