# N1 simplified nose - print this part only

22 September 2026. This nose-only revision supersedes the Rev2 A59 nose and its cover-fastening instructions. The rest of the aircraft is awaiting simplification. Do not order the old 42-piece print set or landing gear.

## File for the printing shop

**A59_Nose_N1_mm.stl - quantity 1 - millimetres - 100% scale.**

Expected bounding dimensions: **109.03 x 309.68 x 88.55 mm** in the supplied XYZ orientation. Rotating the part may exchange these axes. Do not scale to fit a printer or split it without a reviewed joint design. Ask the shop to confirm that the actual part, supports and brim fit its machine. A standard 220 mm bed cannot hold it lengthwise in the supplied orientation.

The STL is a hollow upper nose cover. Its open underside and rear edge meet the wooden frame and lower foam covering; they are intentional. Do not fill the hollow interior or cap these assembly openings. There is no large front service notch and no added cover-screw holes. The original curved tip is restored, making the part about 6.23 mm longer than the notched Rev2 STL. The original rear mating surface is retained.

## Printing

Use the available PLA+ for this nonstructural cover, subject to the shop's material/process judgement. It is not a landing skid, battery restraint or equipment support. Protect it from hot storage and direct impact.

Starting slicing case: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 2 perimeters, 4 top and bottom layers, 5% infill. The shell has original variable wall thickness; do not use vase mode. These settings are a starting point, not machine-specific instructions. The shop chooses orientation, supports and brim, checks every layer and confirms support removal from the cavity. Record quoted filament including supports and finished part mass separately. No machine G-code is supplied.

Reference PrusaSlicer comparison: 327.64 g including supports in supplied orientation, or 228.55 g with the rear opening down and nose tip up, including automatic supports and an 8 mm brim. For the upright case rotate -90 degrees about supplied X; the part is about 310 mm tall on a 109 x 89 mm footprint. Confirm usable build height, brim clearance and tall-print stability. These generic estimates are not a shop quotation or finished cover weight.

Check dimensions and layer bonding after printing. Remove supports without gouging the edges. Dry-fit before drilling, painting or taping. Do not resize the aircraft to compensate for process errors.

## Simple attachment and access

Retain the original A59 mating envelope in the upcoming frame/foam redesign. The frame and lower skin provide the seating edges. Fit small bonded plywood or foamboard locating ledges where needed after the frame is dry-assembled. These are bench-fitted supports, not additional printed columns.

Retain the cover with replaceable reinforced tape bridging the nose/adjacent skin joints. Prepare compatible clean surfaces and test adhesion on the actual plastic and paper. Check lifting, sliding and peel resistance before use. Do not permanently glue the cover shut: lifting it provides electronics and battery access. No nose-cover bolts, I00 spacers, I05 columns or I06 shaped washers are required for this attachment concept.

Keep battery restraint and electronics mounts attached to the wooden structure. Nothing heavy should hang from this cover. Its existing internal equipment envelope remains available; final simplified supports are deferred to the wider redesign.

## Pitot finishing operation

The nose is deliberately supplied without a guessed pitot hole. Once the actual probe and bracket are positioned, mark the passage and drill/file only the required clearance. Support the shell while drilling and deburr it. Keep all probe pressure openings clear of the shell, tape and adhesive. The probe has its own support on the frame; do not rely on a loose hole in this cover as its mount.

The old I14 bracket/notch arrangement is superseded. Check cover removal with the actual probe: if the probe is frame-mounted through a closed hole, make it withdrawable before lifting the cover. Keep pressure tubes accessible. Do not recreate the large Rev2 access notch. Probe placement and retention must be resolved during installation, before flight; they do not require changing this printed blank.

## Checks and scope

The STL has one closed manifold mesh, 32,174 triangles and is approximately 1.61 MB. The enclosed check files record dimensions, CAD comparison, sampled surface deviation, selected equipment-envelope clearances and a reference slicer result. The rendered previews show the actual exported mesh.

This release fixes the nose print geometry only. Landing gear is removed from the intended new design. Belly protection, remaining mounts, controls, frame/foam changes, final mass/CG and complete aircraft instructions are not yet redesigned. Rev2 drawings and calculations remain historical references, not approval to fabricate the rest. Original source design credit remains with the creator linked in the repository README.
