# Print files

Most parts are already printed. The three missed unchanged source files below are supplied one each; reuse other completed prints after inspection.

| Quantity | Part and file | Source reference settings |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [A23 left fixed aileron hinge rail](Individual/A23_3W_15GYR_NOSUP_L0p2_B8.stl) | 3 walls, 15% gyroid, 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, supports off, 8 mm brim |
| 1 | [A36 right fixed aileron hinge rail](Individual/A36_3W_15GYR_NOSUP_L0p2_B8.stl) | Same as A23 |
| 1 | [HTRB right tail horn backing plate](Individual/HTRB_3W_100RECT_NOSUP_L0p2_B5.stl) | 3 walls, 100% rectilinear, 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, supports off, 5 mm brim |

The source recipes specify PLA+. The shop has PLA available; use its actual spool's recommended temperature/cooling and review the slicer preview. Do not invent an ASA requirement or equate ordinary PLA to a qualified PLA+ structural material. A23/A36 are supplied with the span upright, approximately 270.2 mm tall: confirm actual printable height and bed/brim clearance at unchanged scale. HTRB lies flat on its broad 22 × 24 mm face, 1.5 mm thick. HTRB is the backing plate, not the tail horn itself. Watertight meshes and unchanged hashes do not establish real fit or load strength.

A11/A21 moving aileron STLs also remain unchanged; retain existing good prints. [Print_Names_and_Settings.csv](../Data/Print_Names_and_Settings.csv) contains the existing reference recipes for every current print. W means wall loops; GYR/RECT mean gyroid/rectilinear; SUP/NOSUP indicate reference supports; L0p2 is 0.2 mm layer height; B is brim width. An STL does not embed these settings.

A26 and A44_L/R are plywood in the current requested R5G cutting set. Their earlier printed-fit geometry and reference settings are preserved in Archive_Fit_Prototypes and are superseded manufacturing options. Do not print them for the current cutting job or treat a previous PLA A26 fit prototype as a qualified motor mount.
